Bedionita Soro

Postdoctoral Researcher
Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, KAIST, Seoul

I work in the Weight-Space Learning Lab with Professor Sung Ju Hwang. My research centers on generative weight-space learning, neural network synthesis, and AutoML: encoding pretrained model weights into structured latent representations, generative modeling over those representations (diffusion, flow matching, autoregressive models, VAEs), and transfer across architectures. The long-term goal is a generative AutoML system that synthesizes high-performing network weights directly from architectural specifications, reducing training cost and making model development more accessible.

I received my PhD from KAIST in February 2026 with the thesis Weight-Space Learning as a Foundation for Deep AutoML.
